# Physical option delivery: purchased costs and fund underlyings

## [GT-ESTG20-075] Paid holder costs and the separate fund-unit transaction

**Sources:** BMF 14.05.2025, GZ IV C 1 - S 2252/00075/016/070,
[Einzelfragen zur Abgeltungsteuer](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Abgeltungsteuer/2025-05-14-einzelfragen-zur-abgeltungsteuer.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2),
Rn. 21–22, 28–29, 26 and 33 (Tier 2); § 20 Abs. 1 Nr. 11 and Abs. 4 Satz 1
EStG, § 16 Abs. 1 and § 19 Abs. 1 Saetze 1–4 InvStG (Tier 1).
The BMF passages were re-read 2026-09-22 on printed pages 10–13. Statutory
texts are reproduced at [GT-INVSTG-001] and [GT-INVSTG-030].

Applicable to private investments in VZ 2023–2025. Rn. 324 applies the BMF
guidance to all open cases; the general option regime begins in VZ 2009 and
the present InvStG regime in VZ 2018. No new commencement date is asserted.

Rn. 21 Saetze 1–2 identifies the purchased call's premium and purchase expenses
as its acquisition costs. Rn. 22's single sentence provides:

> "Uebt der Inhaber die Kaufoption aus und wird der Basiswert geliefert, gehoeren
> die Anschaffungs- und Anschaffungsnebenkosten des Optionsrechts zu den
> Anschaffungskosten des Basiswerts."

Thus a purchased call's paid acquisition cost increases the delivered asset's
cost. A purchased put's cost reduces the delivered asset's disposal result
under Rn. 28 Saetze 1–2 and Rn. 29 Saetze 1–2, reproduced at [GT-ESTG20-070].
These amounts retain their original acquisition EUR values; physical exercise
does not revalue the historical option costs ([GT-ESTG20-022]). A later sale
of the delivered asset retains that cost even across an intervening year-end.

Rn. 22 refers to the delivered **Basiswert**, not only shares. For investment
fund units, § 19 Abs. 1 Satz 1 InvStG expressly applies § 20 Abs. 4 EStG to
the gain calculation. Consequently the holder's acquisition/disposal cost
also enters the fund-unit result. The further rules of § 19 Abs. 1 remain:
Satz 2 excludes § 20 Abs. 4a, and Saetze 3–4 deduct assessed Vorabpauschalen
in full before any applicable partial exemption ([GT-INVSTG-030]).

Conversely, the **writer's received premium** remains income under § 20
Abs. 1 Nr. 11, separate from physical delivery (Rn. 26 Satz 2 / Rn. 33
Satz 2; [GT-ESTG20-004]). Having a fund as underlying does not turn that
premium into an Investmentertrag. Section 16 Abs. 1 InvStG's closed list
covers distributions, Vorabpauschalen and gains from fund units; a premium
for granting an option is none of these ([GT-INVSTG-001]). The option premium
therefore receives no fund partial exemption. The delivered fund units keep
their own InvStG treatment and declaration destination.

This rule is confined to physical delivery. Rn. 23 and 30 instead govern
holder cash settlement, and Rn. 26 Satz 3 / Rn. 34 writer cash settlement.
Rn. 24/31 governs sale of purchased options and Rn. 27/32 their expiry.
Existing year-specific KAP and KAP-INV destinations apply; no new form line
or rate is introduced by the delivery rule.
